# AgentSuite

Register: brand (specification and long-form documentation).

## What
AgentSuite is an open, content-addressed format for defining agents, instructions, tools, compositions and evaluation cases as OCI artifacts. This site is the format's home: specification, schemas, Go package, conformance fixtures and releases.

## Readers
Platform engineers and runtime/tool builders evaluating or implementing the format; developers arriving from kmx, agentsessions or Orka links.

## Tone
Precise, calm, credible, open-source infrastructure. Reference OCI, OpenTelemetry and CNCF specification sites, not startup landing pages. Neutral member of the agent-primitives family (suite, sessions, canvas), without vendor branding.

## Anti-references
Generic AI-product gradients, marketing hero copy, stock illustrations, emoji, customer or event names, personal names. Preserve specification semantics, heading anchors and raw schema download URLs. Self-host fonts, with no runtime third-party trackers or CDNs.
